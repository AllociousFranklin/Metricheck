Statutory Guide

          
        

        
Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

        
Statutory requirements enforced under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 for all pre-packaged commodities sold in India.


        

          

            
📋 Mandatory Declarations (Rule 6)

            
    
              
  • Rule 6(1)(a): Name & complete address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer.
    • 
              
  • Rule 6(1)(b): Generic / common name of the commodity contained in the package.
    • 
              
  • Rule 6(1)(c): Net quantity declared in standard metric units (g, kg, ml, L, or N).
    • 
              
  • Rule 6(1)(d): Month & year of manufacture or pre-packing.
    • 
              
  • Rule 6(1)(e): Maximum Retail Price (MRP) including "inclusive of all taxes".
    • 
              
  • Rule 6(2): Consumer care contact details (Name/Office, Tel, Email, Postal Address).
    • 
            

          


          

            
🚫 Prohibited Practices (Rule 12 & 13)

            
    
              
  • Vague qualifiers such as "when packed", "approximate", or "net mass" are strictly prohibited.
    • 
              
  • Non-metric counting terms like "dozen" or "gross" are prohibited for specified commodities.
    • 
            

          


          

            
📸 Scanning Best Practices

            
    
              
  • Lighting: Ensure even ambient lighting without direct glare reflections.
    • 
              
  • Contrast: Place product against a plain background for optimal edge detection.
    • 
              
  • Angles: Rotate smoothly to capture all 4 sides, top, and bottom.
